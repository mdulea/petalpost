About Petalpost

Petalpost makes it quick to send a good-looking invitation to the people you want around you.

Why it exists

Most invitation sites ask for an account before you can see your own card, then fill it with upsells. Printed cards look lovely but take weeks. Petalpost sits in between: pick a hand-drawn design, type your details and send a link in a minute, with nothing to sign up for.

How it works

There are hand-drawn templates for birthdays, weddings, baby showers, brunches, graduations and housewarmings. Every design shows an illustration until you add your own photo.

In the editor you add names, a date, a time, a venue, an address and a short note, and choose one of six accent colors. The preview updates as you type. When the card is ready you can download it as an image, copy a link that shows it to anyone, or publish it to collect replies. Guests open the link on any phone, add the event to their calendar and find the address on a map. A host who publishes gets a private page with every reply and the guest totals.

What we do with your data

As little as we can. There are no accounts and no cookies. Your draft and your photo stay in your browser, and your photo is never uploaded. Fonts and scripts are served from our own domain, so no page loads anything from a third party. The privacy page lists every place your data goes.

Help with your invitation

Not sure what to write? The invitation wording guide goes through every line of the card, and when to send invitations covers how much notice to give and when to ask for replies.
