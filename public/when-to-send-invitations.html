When to send invitations

How much notice to give for each kind of event, and when to ask guests to reply.

Send too early and guests forget; too late and they already have plans. These are the usual windows. Give more notice when guests travel, during holidays, or in a busy season such as graduations and summer weddings.

Timing by occasion

Occasion Send the invitation Ask for replies by
Birthday 2 to 3 weeks before; 6 weeks for a milestone with travel 1 week before
Wedding 6 to 8 weeks before, after a save-the-date 6 to 8 months out 3 to 4 weeks before
Baby shower 4 to 6 weeks before 1 to 2 weeks before
Brunch 2 weeks before; 3 to 4 weeks for a restaurant or holiday A few days before the booking deadline
Graduation 3 to 4 weeks before 1 week before
Housewarming 2 to 3 weeks before Optional for an open house; 1 week before for a dinner

Choosing a reply date

Work backwards from your last decision. If the caterer needs numbers ten days ahead, ask for replies two weeks ahead. If you are baking the cake yourself, a few days is enough. Always write an actual date, such as "RSVP by December 5", rather than "soon".

Reminders

A short reminder a day or two after the reply date catches the people who meant to answer. A second one, two or three days before the event, with the time and address, helps everyone who said yes. A digital invite makes this easy: send the same link again with a one-line message.

Save-the-dates

A save-the-date only asks guests to keep the day free. Send one when guests need to book travel or time off: weddings, destination birthdays and big reunions. Six to eight months is usual, and up to a year for a destination wedding. Everyday parties do not need one.

Digital invitations

A link arrives instantly, so you can send it closer to the date than a printed card. It also lets guests add the event to their calendar and open the address on a map in one tap, which cuts down on "what time was it again?" messages. Send it in a personal message rather than a group chat when the guest list is not everyone in the group.
