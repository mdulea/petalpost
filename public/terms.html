Terms of service

Petalpost is a free tool for making and sharing invitations. These are the rules for using it.

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

What Petalpost is

Petalpost lets you design an invitation in your browser. If you publish it, Petalpost stores the text, the template and the color on its server, so guests can open the invitation link and send replies. Petalpost has no accounts. A private link is the only proof that an invitation or a reply is yours.

Your content

You are responsible for what you publish and for the replies you collect. You keep all rights to your text. Publishing gives Petalpost permission to store it and show it to anyone who has the invitation link, and for no other purpose.

What is not allowed

Do not publish an invitation that:

  • is illegal in Romania or in the European Union, or promotes illegal activity;
  • threatens, harasses or incites hatred or violence against a person or a group;
  • shows or sexualizes a child;
  • infringes someone else's copyright, trademark or privacy;
  • contains personal data of a person who did not agree to its publication;
  • is spam, a scam or an attempt to spread malware or collect passwords;
  • tries to overload, probe or break Petalpost.

Reports and removals

Petalpost does not read invitations in advance. A person, not an automated system, handles every report, and Petalpost uses no automated tool to find or remove content.

To report an invitation, use "Report this invitation" under the invitation or write to contact@petalpost.ro with the invitation link, the reason you think it is illegal, your name and email address, and a statement that you act in good faith. Petalpost confirms receipt and decides without undue delay.

When Petalpost removes an invitation, it tells the host why, which rule or law applies and how to contest the decision, if it can reach the host. Petalpost has no host email address, so this is possible only when the host writes first. A host can contest a removal by writing to the same address with the invitation link. A person reviews that message, and the decision stays open to the courts.

Free and as is

Petalpost is free and has no income. It is provided as is, without a promise that it is always available or free of errors. Petalpost may change, limit or stop the service. The privacy page explains when Petalpost deletes invitations. Download your invitation as an image if you want to keep it. Nothing in these terms limits rights that the law gives you and that cannot be waived.

Contact

Users and the authorities of the European Union and its member states reach Petalpost at contact@petalpost.ro, in English or Romanian. Petalpost is run by Marius Dulea.
