Privacy

Petalpost has no accounts and no cookies. This page lists every place your data goes.

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

Who is responsible

Marius Dulea, a private person, runs Petalpost and is the controller of the personal data described on this page. Write to contact@petalpost.ro for any question about your data.

What you type and upload

The editor runs in your browser. Your invite text stays there until you publish the invitation. Your photo is never sent to us.

Your photo is saved in your browser's local storage so it survives a reload. It stays on your device. Remove it with "Use the illustration instead" in the editor or by clearing site data in your browser.

A share link stores the invite text, template and color after the # in the link. Browsers do not send that part of a link to any server. Anyone who has the link can read the invite, so share it only with people you invite.

Published invitations and replies

When a host clicks "Publish", Petalpost stores the invitation text, the template and the accent color. The photo and its position stay in the host's browser.

When a guest replies, Petalpost stores the name the guest enters, the answer, the party size and the note, if the guest writes one. A reply can include a note of up to 300 characters, which the host sees with the reply. The note is optional, so a guest who writes a health detail in it, such as an allergy, chooses to share it with the host.

Petalpost also stores when each invitation and each reply was created and last changed.

Petalpost limits how many invitations one connection can publish per day. For that limit it stores a count with a hash of the IP address and a random value that Petalpost makes each day. During that day, someone with access to the database could find the address by hashing every possible address with the random value. Counts and random values from earlier days are deleted when the next invitation is published.

Private links

Publishing gives the host a management link. Anyone with it can see every reply and delete the invitation. Replying gives the guest an edit link. Anyone with it can see and change that one reply. Anyone with the invitation link can read the invitation and send a reply.

The secret part of a private link comes after the #. Browsers do not send that part of an address to any server. The page sends the secret in a request header to prove access, and Petalpost stores only a hash of it. Petalpost therefore cannot recover a lost link.

When the host clicks "Publish", the editor saves the management secret in the browser's local storage, so the editor can publish changes after a reload. Clearing site data in the browser removes it. The management link still works if the host saved it. The page saves nothing in a guest's browser, but the edit link stays in the address bar and in the browser history.

Why Petalpost may process this data

  • Publishing an invitation and storing replies is necessary to provide the service the host asked for and to deliver each guest's reply to the host. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR.
  • The daily publish limit uses a hash of your IP address. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in preventing abuse, so that the free service stays available to everyone. That is Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR.
  • Vercel's request logs rest on our legitimate interest in the security and operation of the site. That is also Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR.
  • Visitor statistics rest on the legitimate interest described under "Visitor statistics".

Deletion and retention

The host deletes the invitation from the management page. That removes the invitation and all its replies. Petalpost also deletes an invitation and all its replies automatically 90 days after the event date. An invitation without an event date is deleted 90 days after it was last changed, and changing an invitation after its event date delays the deletion. A guest can change a reply but cannot delete it, so ask the host to delete the invitation, or email contact@petalpost.ro, if you need your reply removed sooner.

Your rights

Petalpost has no accounts, so the only proof that data is yours is its private link. A host with the management link sees the invitation and every reply, and can correct or delete them. A guest with the edit link sees, changes and receives their own reply. Those links give you a copy of your data without asking anyone.

For anything else, write to contact@petalpost.ro. You can ask for access, a copy, correction, deletion, restriction and a portable format, and you can object to processing that rests on our legitimate interest. Include the invitation link, and the private link if you have it. With the private link, Petalpost answers in full or deletes the data. With only the invitation link and your name, Petalpost deletes the reply under that name but does not show what any reply says, because a name does not prove who is asking. Without the invitation link, Petalpost cannot find your data. This follows Article 11 of the GDPR. We answer within one month.

You can complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP, at dataprotection.ro, or to the authority of your own country.

Petalpost makes no automated decisions about you and does no profiling.

Storage

Published invitations, replies and the daily counts are stored in a Postgres database run by Neon, which Petalpost uses through Vercel's Neon integration. Only Petalpost's server code connects to it.

Transfers outside the EU

The server code runs at Vercel in Washington, D.C., and the database runs at Neon in the same city. Published invitations, replies and request logs therefore leave the European Economic Area for the United States.

Transfers to Vercel rest on its certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission recognizes as adequate. Transfers to Neon rest on the Standard Contractual Clauses in Neon's data processing addendum. Write to contact@petalpost.ro for a copy of the addendum.

Hosting

Vercel Inc. hosts the site and runs the server code that stores published invitations and replies. When your browser requests a page, Vercel receives your IP address and browser details, because it needs them to deliver the page. Vercel keeps request logs for a short period for security and operation. Vercel acts as our processor under its data processing addendum. Vercel is in the United States and is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Visitor statistics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. Vercel identifies a visit with a hash of the request that resets every 24 hours and does not store your IP address. We see only totals, such as page views, referrers, countries and browser types. The page addresses sent to analytics leave out the part after the #, so private links never reach it.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing which pages people use (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Links to other services

The "Open map" button on an invite opens Google Maps with the event address. Google receives the address and your request only if you click it, under Google's privacy policy.

Fonts and scripts are served from our own domain. No page loads anything from Google or another third party.
