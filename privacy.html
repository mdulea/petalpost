Privacy

Petalpost has no accounts, no database and no cookies. This page lists every place your data goes.

Last updated: 29 October 2026.

What you type and upload

The editor runs in your browser. Your invite text and photo are not sent to us.

Your photo is saved in your browser's local storage so it survives a reload. It stays on your device. Remove it with "Use the illustration instead" in the editor or by clearing site data in your browser.

A share link stores the invite text, template and color after the # in the link. Browsers do not send that part of a link to any server. Anyone who has the link can read the invite, so share it only with people you invite.

Hosting

Vercel Inc. hosts the site. When your browser requests a page, Vercel receives your IP address and browser details, because it has to in order to deliver the page. Vercel keeps request logs for a short period for security and operation. Vercel acts as our processor under its data processing addendum. Vercel is in the United States and is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Visitor statistics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. Vercel identifies a visit with a hash of the request that resets every 24 hours and does not store your IP address. We see only totals, such as page views, referrers, countries and browser types.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing which pages people use (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Links to other services

The "Open map" button on an invite opens Google Maps with the event address. Google receives the address and your request only if you click it, under Google's privacy policy.

Fonts and scripts are served from our own domain. No page loads anything from Google or another third party.
