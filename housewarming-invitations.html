What to put on a housewarming invitation

  • The new address. This is the one detail guests cannot guess. The invite link gives them a map button.
  • Parking and entry. A buzzer code, which floor, or where to park.
  • Date and time. An open house with a time window suits a home where not everyone fits at once.
  • Gifts. Guests often bring something. If you would rather they did not, say "no gifts, just you".
  • Pets and children. If you have a pet, mention it for guests with allergies.

When to send it

Hold the party once the boxes are unpacked, usually one to three months after moving in. Send invitations two to three weeks ahead. The card doubles as your change-of-address note, so you can send it to people who cannot come too.
